\newcommand{\figcapturetimingcaption}{Capture contract for the first live radar session. The runner enters standby, applies the requested settings, enables TX, waits for the acknowledged activation, reads the settings after activation, and allows at least 5 s for settling before recording each condition for $\geq 30$ s. It then stops TX, performs a fresh post-TX validation, and returns to standby. UDP recording begins at the Record state and branches independently to the raw archive and decoder; metadata from the post-activation readback is persisted with the CSV/PNG outputs. The smoke test queried seven settings, archived 1,024 UDP payloads, decoded three rotations, and measured 10.008123 s, so its figure documents the transport smoke test while the $\geq 30$-s interval defines the campaign contract.}

\newcommand{\figsettingscoveragecaption}{Settings command/readback coverage across the functional test sequence. The recovered source contains 241 outcomes, including 205 accepted, 34 clamped, and two superseded records; the superseded interference-rejection records are retained as a separate category because they require the corrected supplement and are not deferred. Missing or unknown outcome labels are excluded and reported separately by the adapter.}
