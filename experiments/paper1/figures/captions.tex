\newcommand{\figcapturetimingcaption}{Capture contract for live sessions. After TX acknowledgement, the runner obtains a fresh verified settings readback and records rotations. The radar multicast feeds independent raw and native receiver sockets. The raw receiver takes timestamps immediately after recvfrom and before queueing, then sends payloads through a bounded FIFO of 16,384 entries to a separate archive writer. The writer stores payloads and session metadata in raw\_udp\_payloads.jsonl. The recorded session captured the 2 MiB request and emitted a final manifest; FIFO overflow or write failure is an explicit session error. The native receiver decodes rotations into a separate bounded 16-job queue for CSV/PNG and PPI preview persistence. Native stop waits up to 45~s for its queue to drain; raw archive shutdown completes with the final manifest.}
